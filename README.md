# 🔬 Medical Laboratory Technology (MLT) Digital Garden

A structured, interconnected digital garden documenting clinical laboratory sciences, diagnostic techniques, quality assurance protocols, and pathology notes.

🌐 **Live Garden:** [View My Digital Garden]([https://your-site-name.vercel.app](https://mlt-digital-garden.vercel.app/))

---

## 📚 Vault Overview

This repository hosts notes and reference sheets written in **Obsidian** and deployed automatically via **Vercel** using the **Obsidian Digital Garden** template.

### Covered Subjects

* **Hematology & Hemostasis** — CBC interpretation, smear examination, coagulation profiles, and bleeding disorders.
* **Clinical Biochemistry** — Routine chemistry panels (LFT, KFT, lipid profiles), spectrophotometry, and electrolyte/acid-base analysis.
* **Medical Microbiology & Parasitology** — Culture techniques, Gram/AFB staining, biochemical differentiation, AST (Kirby-Bauer), and stool/blood examination.
* **Histopathology & Cytology** — Tissue processing, embedding, sectioning, H&E staining, and cytological smears.
* **Immunohematology & Blood Banking** — Blood grouping, crossmatching, antibody screening, and transfusion protocols.
* **Laboratory Safety & Quality Control** — Biomedical waste management (BMW), Westgard rules, and Levey-Jennings quality charts.
