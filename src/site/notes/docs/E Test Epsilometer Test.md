## E-test (Epsilometer Test)
![Pasted image 20260927091138.png\|483](/img/user/Attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260927091138.png)
E-test is a quantitative agar diffusion method in which an antimicrobial gradient strip is placed on an inoculated agar plate, and the MIC is read where the ellipse of inhibition intersects the strip.

### Principle
A plastic strip contains a **continuous exponential gradient** of a specific antimicrobial concentration. When the strip is placed on an agar plate inoculated with the test organism, the drug diffuses into the agar and produces an elliptical zone of inhibition

The **MIC** is read directly from the scale on the strip at the point where the lower edge of the inhibition ellipse intersects the strip.

### Interpretation
- **Low MIC:** organism is more likely to be susceptible to that antimicrobial.
- **High MIC:** organism is more likely to be resistant.
- The measured MIC is compared with accepted susceptibility breakpoints to report the organism as **susceptible, intermediate/increased exposure, or resistant**.


what is defination of minimum inhibitory concentration MIC

**Minimum inhibitory concentration (MIC)** is the **lowest concentration of an antimicrobial agent**—such as an antibiotic or antifungal—that **prevents visible growth of a microorganism in vitro after a specified incubation period**. It is commonly expressed in **µg/mL** or **mg/L**.
.
**Important:** MIC shows **inhibition of visible growth**, not necessarily killing of the organism. The concentration that kills bacteria is called the **minimum bactericidal concentration (MBC)**
