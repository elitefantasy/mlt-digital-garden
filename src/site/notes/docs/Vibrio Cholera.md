[[docs/Gram Negative Bacilli\|Gram Negative Bacilli]]


### 1. Taxonomy & Classification
- **Gram-Negative Bacillus**:
- **Capsule**:  **non-capsulated**

### 2. Morphology & Motility
- **Motility**: It is motile via a **single polar flagellum** (monotrichous flagellar arrangement).

### 3. Pathogenesis & Clinical Features
- **Causative Agent of Cholera**: 
- **Enterotoxin Action**: It secretes an **exotoxin (enterotoxin)** that leads to severe fluid secretion into the gut.
- **"Rice Water" Stool**: The hallmark stool presentation is **"rice water" stool**—described as copious, thin, watery stool containing white flakes.
- **Serotypes**: Strains such as **O1 and O139** represent the primary pathogenic variants responsible for outbreaks.

1. **Enrichment Media (Liquid)**
	- **Alkaline Peptone Water (APW)**: _Vibrio cholerae_
2. **Selective and Differential Media (Solid)**
	- **TCBS Agar (Thiosulfate Citrate Bile Salts Sucrose Agar)**: The main selective solid medium for isolating _Vibrio cholerae_. Since _Vibrio cholerae_ ferments sucrose, it converts the green TCBS agar medium into distinct **yellow colonies**.

3. **Transport Media**
