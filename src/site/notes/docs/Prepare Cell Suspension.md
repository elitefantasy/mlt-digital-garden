How to prepare a cell suspension

Formula   
 RBC Volume = ( Percentage / 100 ) * Total Volume

Saline(solution) \= Total Volume \- RBC Volume

---

| RBC | Saline | Suspension |
| :-- | :----- | :--------- |
| 2   | 98     | 2%         |
| 3   | 97     | 3%         |
| 5   | 95     | 5%         |
| 10  | 90     | 10%        |
