# Comparison of *Neisseria meningitidis* and *Neisseria gonorrhoeae*

**Morphology**
  * Both are Gram-negative diplococci, catalase-positive, and oxidase-positive.  
  * *Neisseria meningitidis*: ==Lens shaped== / half-moon shaped diplococci; **Capsulated** (12 serogroups: A, B, C, X, Y, Z, W-135, 29E, H, I, K, L); capsule is primary virulence factor.  
  * *Neisseria gonorrhoeae*: ==Kidney-bean shaped== / coffee-bean shaped diplococci; ==Non-capsulated==; pili/fimbriae are primary virulence factors.  

**Culture Media:**  
  * Non-hemolytic (gamma-hemolytic) on blood agar and chocolate agar.
  * **Selective Media (Both):** **Thayer-Martin Medium** (Blood agar \+ Vancomycin, Colistin, Nystatin), **Modified Thayer-Martin Medium** (+ Trimethoprim), and **Martin-Lewis Medium**.  
**Sugar Fermentation:**  
  * *Neisseria gonorrhoeae* (**G**): ==Ferments **G**lucose only.==  
  * *Neisseria meningitidis* (**M**): Ferments **M**altose and **G**lucose.  
**Clinical Pathogenesis:**  
  * *Neisseria meningitidis*: Pyogenic meningitis (fever, headache, neck stiffness, seizures, petechial rash) and fulminant meningococcemia with bilateral adrenal hemorrhage (**Waterhouse-Friderichsen Syndrome**).  
  * *Neisseria gonorrhoeae*:  
    * Sexually Transmitted Infection (STI).  
    * **Males:** Acute purulent anterior urethritis; untreated leads to epididymitis and "watercan perineum".  
    * **Females:** Mucopurulent cervicitis; untreated ascends to cause Pelvic Inflammatory Disease (PID) and Fitz-Hugh-Curtis Syndrome (perihepatitis).  
    * **Pre-pubertal Girls:** Vulvovaginitis (due to alkaline prepubertal vaginal pH).  
    * **Infants:** **Ophthalmia Neonatorum** (bilateral purulent conjunctivitis contracted during birth through an infected maternal birth canal during vaginal delivery).
