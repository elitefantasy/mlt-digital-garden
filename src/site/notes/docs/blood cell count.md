# Hemocytometer / Neubauer chamber
- Invented by Louis-Charles Malassez
-  the depth (clearance) between the grid and the cover slip is strictly **0.1 mm**.
- The total counting area is a 3 mm x 3 mm square, resulting in a total area of 9 mm².
- This area is divided into **9 large squares**, each measuring 1 mm x 1 mm. 
- **WBC Counting Area:** The **4 corner squares** are designated for WBC counting. Each of these 1 mm x 1 mm corner squares is further subdivided into 16 smaller squares. 
- **RBC/Platelet Counting Area:** The **1 central square** is designated for RBCs, platelets, and sperm. It is subdivided into 25 smaller squares (each measuring 0.2 mm x 0.2 mm). Each of these 25 squares is further subdivided into 16 tiny squares, yielding 400 total tiny squares in the central area.
![Pasted image 20260815190324.png](/img/user/Attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260815190324.png)

* **Total Chamber Volume:** Length (3 mm) x Width (3 mm) x Depth (0.1 mm) \= **0.9 mm³** (or 0.9 µL, since 1 mm³ \= 1 µL).  
* **One WBC Corner Square Volume:** 1 mm x 1 mm x 0.1 mm \= **0.1 mm³** (0.1 µL).  
* **One RBC Small Square (1 of 25\) Volume:** 0.2 mm x 0.2 mm x 0.1 mm \= 0.004 mm³.




# 1. WBC Count /TLC
- EDTA sample
- **WBC Pipette Characteristics:** it is a type of thoma pipette , Has markings at 0.5, 1, and 11\. It contains a **white bead** inside the bulb which aids in mixing, identifies it as a WBC pipette, and indicates if the pipette is dry (bead rolls freely) or wet (bead sticks).
- ![Pasted image 20260816182614.png\|242](/img/user/Attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260816182614.png)
- **Diluting Fluid (==Turk's Fluid==):** Drawn up to the **11 mark**, creating a **1:20 dilution factor**.

**Composition of turks fluid is**
?
* **Glacial Acetic Acid (1.5% to 2%):** Lyses the red blood cells, which would otherwise obscure the WBCs due to their vastly higher numbers (approx. 5 million vs 7,000).
* **Gentian Violet or Methyl Violet (1%):** Colors the fluid and stains the nuclei of the white blood cells to make them easily visible.
* **Distilled Water (97%):** Acts as the solvent.

## Procedure
* Discard the first few drops from the pipette as they mostly contain unmixed diluting fluid.  
* Charge the hemocytometer via capillary action by placing the pipette tip at the edge of the cover slip. 
* Allow cells to settle undisturbed for 1-2 minutes. Count under 10x or 40x magnification.  
* Count cells in the 4 corner squares using the **L-Rule/Margin Rule**.  
* **Formula:** WBC/mm³ \= (Number of cells counted x Dilution Factor) / (Area counted x Depth).  
* **Simplified Formula:** Total WBC Count \= **N x 50** (where N is the number of cells counted in the 4 corner squares). *(Derivation: N x 20 dilution / (4 squares x 0.1 mm depth) \= N x 20 / 0.4 \= N x 50\)*.

# 2. DLC | Differential Leucocyte Count

- smear prepare and stain using leishman stain
* A minimum of **100 cells** must be counted and tallied in a 10x10 grid box system to derive the relative percentage.


**1. Absolute vs. Relative Count:**

* Relative count is the percentage derived directly from the DLC (e.g., 60% Neutrophils).  
* Absolute count reveals the true state of the patient's immune response.   
  * **Formula:** Absolute Count \= (Relative Percentage / 100\) x TLC.   

# 3. Reticulocyte Count
- Reticulocytes are immature RBCs containing remnants of ribosomal RNA (rRNA).
- this test tells about bone marrow erythropoietic activity
- takes approximately ==**2 days**== for a reticulocyte to fully mature into a mature RBC.

**Counting**
- Counting is ideally done using a **Phase Contrast Microscope** or by inserting an **Ehrlich eyepiece** (with a 4mm x 4mm adjustable cardboard diaphragm) to limit the field of view.  
* A minimum of **100 reticulocytes** should be counted across multiple fields alongside the total RBCs in those same fields.  
* **Formula for Percentage:** (Number of Reticulocytes / Total RBCs in examined fields) x 100\.  
* **Formula for Absolute Count:** (Reticulocyte % x RBC Count). 

**Automated Methods**
* Utilizes flow cytometry with fluorescent dyes like **Acridine Orange, Auramine O, Thiazole Orange**, or non-fluorescent dyes like **Oxazine**.
* RNA fluoresces ==1;;**orange-red**== while DNA fluoresces ==1;;**yellow**==. Fluorescence intensity is directly proportional to RNA content. Routine manual fluorescence microscopy is rarely used due to rapid signal fading upon light exposure.

# 4. RBC Count
- uses thoma pipette(RBC pipette), red bead, 0.5, 1, and 101 marks, **1:200 dilution**
- Diluting fluids include **Hayem's fluid** (mercuric chloride, sodium chloride, sodium sulfate)
- Cells are counted in the **5 small squares** of the central grid (4 corners \+ 1 center)
- **Calculation Formula:** Total RBC Count \= **N x 10,000** (where N \= cells counted in 5 squares).

# 5. Platelate Count
- RBC pipette or WBC pipette (Dacie & Lewis recommend the WBC pipette for a 1:20 dilution).
- Preferred diluting fluid: ==**1% Ammonium Oxalate**.==

# Normal Reference Range

* **Total Leukocyte Count (Adults):** 4,000 to 11,000 cells/mm³.  
	* *Newborns:* 10,000 \- 25,000 /mm³.  
	* *Children (8-12 yrs):* 4,500 \- 13,500 /mm³.  
* **Differential Leukocyte Count (Adult Relative %):**  
	* Neutrophils: 40 \- 75%.  
	* Lymphocytes: 20 \- 40%.  
	* Monocytes: 2 \- 10%.  
	* Eosinophils: 1 \- 6%.  
	* Basophils: 0 \- 1% (\<1%).  
* **Reticulocyte Count (Adults):** 0.5% to 2.5% of total RBCs.  
	* **Reticulocyte Count (Newborns/Cord Blood):** 2% to 6%.  
	* **Absolute Reticulocyte Count:** 25,000 to 75,000 cells/mm³.
