Rheumatoid Arthritis is a chronic, systemic autoimmune disorder causing symmetric inflammatory polyarthritis. 

## 1. Key Diagnostic Biomarkers
- **Rheumatoid Factor (RF):**
    - _Nature:_ Autoantibody (predominantly **IgM**) directed against the **Fc region of human IgG**.
    - _Sensitivity:_ 60%–80%.
    - _Specificity:_ Low (~70%–80%); can be elevated in other conditions (SLE, Sjögren syndrome, chronic infections, elderly).
- **Anti-Cyclic Citrullinated Peptide (Anti-CCP / ACPA):**
    - _Nature:_ Autoantibodies directed against citrullinated arginine residues in joint peptides.
    - _Sensitivity:_ 65%–80% (detectable in early RA).
    - _Specificity:_ High (>95%); standard for early diagnosis and prognosis.
**2. Laboratory Methods**


| **Test**                        | **Primary Methodology**     | **Key Principle / Reporting**                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **RF (Qualitative/Semi-quant)** | Latex Agglutination         | Polystyrene latex coated with human ==IgG== agglutinates in the presence of RF. Reported as positive/negative or titer (e.g., $\ge$ 1:80). |
| **RF (Quantitative)**           | Nephelometry / Turbidimetry | Measures light scatter/attenuation by antigen-antibody complexes. Reported in IU/mL (positive usually $>20\text{ IU/mL}$).                 |
| **Anti-CCP**                    | ELISA / CLIA                | Microplate or magnetic beads coated with synthetic CCP. Reported in units (e.g., positive $>20\text{ U/mL}$).                              |

**3. Supportive Inflammatory Markers**
- **ESR (Erythrocyte Sedimentation Rate):** Assessed via Westergren method; elevated during active synovitis.
- **CRP (C-Reactive Protein):** Quantitative acute-phase reactant; responds faster than ESR to therapeutic response or flare-ups.
**4. Specimen Requirements & Sources of Error**
- **Sample:** Serum (red top or SST). Centrifuge after complete clot retraction.
- **Storage:** 2°C–8°C for up to 48 hours; freeze at -20°C for longer storage. Avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles.
- **Interferences:**
    - Severe lipemia or gross hemolysis can cause false-positive turbidity readings in nephelometry.
    - **Prozone phenomenon:** High antibody titers can cause false-negative latex agglutination (dilute sample to retest).
