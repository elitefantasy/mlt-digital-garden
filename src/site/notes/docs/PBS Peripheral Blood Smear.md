**Characteristic of Good Smear**
- Smooth and tongue-shaped.
- Gradually thinner toward the feathered edge.
- Free of streaks, ridges, holes, and clumps.
- Evenly distributed.
- Not too thick or too thin.
- Properly stained.

## RBC
- Red Blood cell
	- biconcave , diameter 7.5 micrometer
	- central pallor
	- normocytic
	- macrocytic : vit b12 deff, folic acid deff, myelodysplastic disorder, acquired sideroblastic anemia
	- microcytic
	- normochromic
	- hypochromic microcytic : IDA , thassemia, hereditary sideroblastic anemia 
	- hyperchromic

Poikilocyte disorders
- *spherocyte*: round, densely staining red cells that lack central pallor and have a smaller than normal diameter
- *stomatocyte* :  area of central pallor is elliptical rather than round, giving the cell the appearance of the opening of a mouth (stoma)
- *target cell* : centrally located disk of hemoglobin , thalassemia, hepatic dx, sickel cell anemia
- *leptocyte* : thin, flat cells with the hemoglobin at the periphery of the cell
- *Sickel Cel*l : elongated, sometimes crescent-shaped, erythrocytes with pointed ends
- *elliptocyte* : slightly oval to elongated cigar-shaped forms, thalessemia, myelofibrosis
- *teardrop* : one end round and the other end more pointed, myelofibrosis
- *acanthocyte* : several irregularly spaced blunted projections from the margin of the cells , alcoholic liver dx, pyruvate kinase deff
- *echinocyte*
- *schistocyte* : hallmark of hemolytic anemia , DIC
- *bite cell* : Heinz Body anemia such as G6PD deff
- *rouleaux formation* : due to high serum protein , multiple myeloma, macroglobulinemia

**Inclusion Bodies**
- Howell–Jolly bodies : DNA remnants , splenecotomis, 
- Basophilic stippling : small coarse or fine blue granules, HB synthesis impaired , lead poising, sideroblastic anemia , thalassemia
- pappenheimer bodies : iron granules
- dohle bodies
- pelger huet : impaired nuclear segmentation, bilobed nucleus

## Important differences
|Feature|Schistocyte|Spherocyte|
|---|---|---|
|Shape|Fragmented, irregular|Round, dense|
|Central pallor|Usually absent|Absent|
|Mechanism|Mechanical fragmentation|Loss of membrane surface|
|Conditions|DIC, TMA, prosthetic valves|Hereditary spherocytosis, autoimmune hemolysis|

|Feature|Left shift|Leukemoid reaction|
|---|---|---|
|Main finding|Increased immature neutrophils|Marked reactive leukocytosis|
|Cause|Infection or inflammation|Severe infection, malignancy, stress|
|Blasts|Usually absent|Usually absent|
|Need for correlation|CBC and clinical findings|CBC, smear, clinical findings, sometimes marrow studies|
