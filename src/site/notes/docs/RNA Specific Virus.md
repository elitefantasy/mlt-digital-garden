# Coxsackie Virus
- **single-stranded RNA virus**
- It is cultivated on :: suckling mice
- These mice are highly susceptible to the Coxsackie virus, allowing for significant viral replication and development within their bodies 
*   **Cytopathic Effects (CPE):** The virus produces different types of cytopathic effects . This means it severely damages the host cells it infects, disrupting their normal formation, shape, and function, which can then be easily visualized and studied 
*   **Significance of using Suckling Mice:** While viral replication might stop at a certain stage when using standard tissue or chick embryo cultures, suckling mice support the complete replication cycle of the Coxsackie virus (from initial infection to the host's death)  This makes suckling mice highly valuable for fully studying the virus, understanding its replication process, and developing treatments
