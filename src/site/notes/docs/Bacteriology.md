# Classification
## classification by gram staining
**Gram Staining Classification | gram negative vs gram positive**

| Feature | Gram-Positive Bacteria | Gram-Negative Bacteria |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Cell Wall Staining | Retains crystal violet stain | Takes up counterstain : safranin |
| Color Under Microscope | Purple / Violet | Red / Pink |
| Peptidoglycan Layer Thickness | Thick | Thin |
| Outer Membrane Presence | Absent | Present |
| **Teichoic Acid** | **Present**  | **Absent** |
| LPS | Absent | Present in some membrane |
| Lipid Content | Low | High |
| Periplasmic Space | Absent or very narrow | Well developed |
| **Porins**  | **Present**  | **Absent**  |
| Sensitivity to penicillin | Generally more sensitive  | Generally **less sensitive** because outer membrane acts as a barrier  |
| endotoxin | Absent | **Present** as lipid A component of LPS  |
| Spore formation | Some form spores, e.g. **Bacillus**, **Clostridium**  | **Usually do not form spores**  |
| Toxic component  | Mainly exotoxins in some species  | **Endotoxin is major toxic component**  |
| Representative Examples | **Cocci:** *Staphylococcus, Streptococcus* **Bacilli:** *Bacillus, Clostridium, Actinomyces* | **Cocci:** *Neisseria, Moraxella* **Bacilli:** *E. coli, Salmonella, Pseudomonas* |

## Classification by Morphology (Shape):

* **Cocci:** Spherical or round-shaped bacterial cells.  
  * **Bacilli:** Rod-shaped bacterial cells.

## classification by Taxonomic Categories:

* **Gram-Positive Cocci (GPC):** Staphylococcus , Streptococcus 
* **Gram-Negative Cocci (GNC):** :: Neisseria , moraxella  
* **Gram-Positive Bacilli (GPB):** ::  **A B C D L** :   actinomyces , Bacillus,  clostridium, cornybacterium diptberiae , Listeria 
* **Gram-Negative Bacilli (GNB):** Red rod-shaped bacteria. Comprises all remaining pathogenic bacilli.

## Core Morphological Mnemonics

### Capsulated Bacteria

 * **Total Number:** Exactly 9 primary medically important capsulated bacteria.  
* **Mnemonic:** **PAKIB MCV**  
  * **P:** *Pneumococcus* (*Streptococcus pneumoniae*)  
  * **A:** *Anthrax* (*Bacillus anthracis*) — Unique polypeptide capsule.  
  * **K:** *Klebsiella pneumoniae*  
  * **I:** *Influenzae* (*Haemophilus influenzae*)  
  * **B:** *Bordetella pertussis*  
  * **M:** *Meningococcus* (*Neisseria meningitidis*)  
  * **C:** *Clostridium* — Specifically *C. perfringens* and *C. tetani*  
  * **V:** *Vibrio parahaemolyticus* (Note: *Vibrio cholerae* is non-capsulated).

### Spore-Forming Bacteria

> * **Total Number:** 4 primary spore-forming bacterial genera.  
> * **Mnemonic:** **BSC Chemistry** (2 B's, S, 2 C's)  
  * **B:** *Bacillus anthracis* and *Bacillus subtilis*  
  * **S:** *Sporosarcina*  
  * **C:** *Clostridium* species  
  * **C:** *Coxiella burnetii*

### Motile Bacteria

> * **Total Number:** 12 primary motile bacteria divided equally by flagellar arrangement (6 Peritrichous \+ 6 Polar).  
> * **Flagellar Arrangements:**  
  * **Peritrichous Flagella:** Flagella distributed uniformly around the entire cell surface.  
  * **Polar Flagella:** Flagella attached at one or both poles/ends of the cell.  
> * **Peritrichous Motile Bacteria (6 Total)**   
  * **C:** *Clostridia* (Exceptions: *Clostridium perfringens* and *Clostridium tetani* type 6 are non-motile).  
  * **B:** *Bacillus* species (Exception: *Bacillus anthracis* is non-motile).  
  * **S:** *Salmonella* species  
  * **L:** *Listeria monocytogenes*  
  * **E:** *Escherichia coli*  
  * **P:** *Proteus* species  
> * **Polar Motile Bacteria (6 Total)V:** *Vibrio cholerae*  
  * **P:** *Pseudomonas aeruginosa*  
  * **S:** *Spirochetes* (e.g., *Treponema*, *Borrelia*, *Leptospira*)  
  * **H:** *Helicobacter pylori*  
  * **C:** *Campylobacter jejuni*  
  * **L:** *Legionella pneumophila*

## Arrangement Characteristics of Pathogenic Bacteria:

* **Cuneiform Arrangement (Chinese Letter Pattern):** Characteristic angular arrangement of :: *Corynebacterium diphtheriae*.  
  * **Bamboo Stick Appearance:** Characteristic chain arrangement of :: *Bacillus anthracis*.

  * **Lancet-Shaped / Flame-Shaped Diplococci:** Paired arrangement typical of :: *Streptococcus pneumoniae* (Pneumococcus).  
  * **Kidney Bean-Shaped Diplococci:** Paired arrangement typical of :: *Neisseria gonorrhoeae* (Gonococcus).

## **Bacterial Ultrastructure & Organelle Functions**

-  **Mesosome Function:** Infoldings of the bacterial cytoplasmic membrane that serve as the site of cellular respiration and energy (ATP) generation in prokaryotes.  
- **Plasmid Definition:** Small, circular, extra-chromosomal double-stranded DNA molecule capable of autonomous replication. 
- **Prokaryotic Ribosome Structure:** 70S ribosome composed of 50S (large) and 30S (small) subunits ('S' stands for Svedberg unit of sedimentation coefficient).  
- **Capsule / Glycocalyx Function:** Protects bacteria from desiccation (drying out) and aids in cell adhesion to surfaces/tissues.  
- **Nucleoid Features:** Single, circular, double-stranded DNA not enclosed by a nuclear membrane (naked genetic material).

## **7\. Lactose Fermenting vs. Non-Lactose Fermenting Bacteria**

> * **Lactose Fermenters (LF):** *Escherichia coli*, *Klebsiella*, *Enterobacter*, and *Citrobacter*.  
> * **Non-Lactose Fermenters (NLF):** *Salmonella*, *Shigella*, *Proteus*, *Pseudomonas*, and *Yersinia*.

“Many gut bacteria are lactose fermenters; in stool and urine cultures, lactose fermentation helps differentiate enteric bacteria on media such as MacConkey agar.”
