**ques:** a buffer is prepared by mixing 0.1M acetic acid (pKa = 4.76) with 0.1M sodium acetate . what is the pH of this buffer ?
?
The pH of this buffer solution is **4.76**.
.
This can be calculated using the **Henderson-Hasselbalch equation**:
$$\text{pH} = \text{p}K_a + \log\left(\frac{[\text{Conjugate Base}]}{[\text{Weak Acid}]}\right)$$
- **$\text{p}K_a$ of acetic acid:** $4.76$
- **$[\text{Conjugate Base}]$ ($\text{CH}_3\text{COO}^-$ from sodium acetate):** $0.1\text{ M}$
- **$[\text{Weak Acid}]$ ($\text{CH}_3\text{COOH}$):** $0.1\text{ M}$
$$\text{pH} = 4.76 + \log\left(\frac{0.1}{0.1}\right)$$
$$\text{pH} = 4.76 + \log(1)$$
Since $\log(1) = 0$:
$$\text{pH} = 4.76$$
Whenever the concentrations of the weak acid and its conjugate base are equal, the pH of the buffer equals the $\text{p}K_a$.
