**ques:** a buffer is prepared by mixing 0.1M acetic acid (pKa = 4.76) with 0.1M sodium acetate . what is the pH of this buffer ?
?
The pH of this buffer solution is **4.76**.
.
This can be calculated using the **Henderson-Hasselbalch equation**:
$$\text{pH} = \text{p}K_a + \log\left(\frac{[\text{Conjugate Base}]}{[\text{Weak Acid}]}\right)$$
- **$\text{p}K_a$ of acetic acid:** $4.76$
- **$[\text{Conjugate Base}]$ ($\text{CH}_3\text{COO}^-$ from sodium acetate):** $0.1\text{ M}$
- **$[\text{Weak Acid}]$ ($\text{CH}_3\text{COOH}$):** $0.1\text{ M}$
$$\text{pH} = 4.76 + \log\left(\frac{0.1}{0.1}\right)$$
$$\text{pH} = 4.76 + \log(1)$$
Since $\log(1) = 0$:
$$\text{pH} = 4.76$$
Whenever the concentrations of the weak acid and its conjugate base are equal, the pH of the buffer equals the $\text{p}K_a$.


**Quest** : How much KCl is required to prepare 500 ml of 150mmol/L KCl Solution ?
.
Given Molar mass of KCl is 74.55
- $150\text{ mmol/L}$ = $\frac{150}{1000}\text{ mol/L}$ = $0.15\text{ mol/L}$
- $500\text{ mL}$ = $\frac{500}{1000}\text{ L}$ = $0.5\text{ L}$
-  $\text{Moles}$ = $\text{Molarity} \times \text{Volume (L)}$ 
- $\text{Moles} = 0.15\text{ mol/L} \times 0.5\text{ L} = 0.075\text{ mol}$`
- $\text{Mass (g)} = \text{Moles} \times \text{Molar Mass}$
- $\text{Mass (g)} = 0.075\text{ mol} \times 74.55\text{ g/mol} = 5.59125\text{ g}$


**Quest**: Find the median of number 5, 7, 9, 2, 3 is
.
 * Arrange the numbers in ascending order:  
 * Count the total observations (n): 5
 * Locate the middle position:
   Since n is an odd number, the median is the value at position $\frac{n + 1}{2}$ = $\frac{5 + 1}{2}$ = $3\text{rd}$ position.
 * Identify the value:
   The 3rd number in the ordered list (2, 3, $\mathbf{5}$, 7, 9) is 5.
