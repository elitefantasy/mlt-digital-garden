Bacterial flora of water 
- natural bacteria of water : pseudomonas, micrococci
- natural soil bacteria : bacillus subtilis , enterobacter
- sewage bacteria : ecoli, salmonella, vibrio , proteus , c.pefrengins 

Other bacteria of water : salmonella typhi , c.jejuni 

Virus : hepatitis e , hepatitis a , poliomyelitis , rotavirus 

Protozoan : E.Histolytica , giardia lambila , cryptosporidium spp, balantidium coli

Helminths : ascaris lumbricoides , trichuris trichuria ,

Factors affecting bacterial growth 
- Salinity : salty water is bactericidal 
- acidity , temp , light , storage , organic matter , types of water
