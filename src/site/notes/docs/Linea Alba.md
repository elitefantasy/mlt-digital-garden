Linea alba (Latin for "white line") most commonly refers to the fibrous band running down the center of the abdomen, separating the "six-pack" muscles

  
Abdominal Linea Alba
- Location :: Runs vertically from the breastbone (xiphoid process) down to the pubic bone.
- Function: Joins the left and right abdominal muscles and helps stabilize your trunk.
- Common Issues: Can stretch or separate during pregnancy or heavy strain (known as diastasis recti), or develop hernias.
- Note: During pregnancy, extra pigment can turn this line dark, when it is called the _linea nigra_.

Oral Linea Alba
- Location: Appears horizontally on the inner lining of the cheek (buccal mucosa), level with where your teeth bite together.
- Cause: Harmless friction, cheek sucking, or pressure from teeth.
- Treatment: None needed; it is similar to a tiny callus on the skin.
