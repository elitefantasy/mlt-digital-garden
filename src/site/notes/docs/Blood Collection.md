## **Specimen Collection, Phlebotomy & Anticoagulants**

### **A. Blood Collection Techniques**

* **Capillary Collection (Peripheral Blood):**  
  * Preferred site in adults: Ring finger of non-dominant hand (less frequent usage).  
  * Technique: Clean site with 70% alcohol/spirit, prick with sterile lancet, *discard the first drop* (contains tissue fluid), collect the second drop.  
  * Infants/Small Children: **Lateral or medial plantar surface of the heel, earlobe, or big toe.**  
  * Applications: Blood grouping, peripheral smears, hemoglobin screening.

**Q) Which of the following is the correct practice when collecting capillary blood from an infant (PYQ)?**

1. a. use the lateral or medial plantar surface of the heel  
2. b. use the same site for repeated collection within 24hr  
3. c. use the centre of the heel to ensure adequate blood flow  
4. d. perform the puncture on the big toe

**Answer: a. Use the lateral or medial plantar surface of the heel.**

* **Venous Blood Collection (Venipuncture):**  
  * Primary site: Median cubital vein in the antecubital fossa of the arm.  
  * Angle of insertion: 15°–45° (optimal: 25°–30°). Syringe volume: 2–5 mL.  
  * Tourniquet Rule: Apply above elbow; do not keep tied for \>1 minute to prevent hemoconcentration/hematoma.  
  * Vacutainer vs. Vial: Vacutainers maintain evacuated vacuum (no air inside); opening the cap converts it into an open vial.  
* **Arterial Collection:** Sites: Radial or femoral artery. Performed by trained clinicians for Arterial Blood Gas (ABG) analysis.  
* **Universal Safety Rule:** Treat all patient specimens as potentially infectious. Wear gloves, lab coat, mask; no mouth pipetting; perform hand washing.

### **B. Anticoagulants & Color Coding**

In sequence of order of draw 

| Color Top | Anticoagulant / Additive | Mechanism / Ratio | Primary Clinical Applications |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Blood culture tube | SPS (Sodium Polyanethol Sulfonate) | Anticoagulant; inhibits phagocytosis | Microbiology (Blood culture) |
| Light Blue | 3.2% Sodium Citrate | Binds calcium; **1:9** (citrate:blood ratio) | Coagulation studies (PT, APTT, Fibrinogen) |
| Red Top | None (Plain Vial) | Allows natural blood clotting | Serum chemistry, serology, cross-matching |
| Yellow Top | SST / Clot Activator Gel / micro silica particle | Separates serum from clot after centrifugation | Biochemistry panels, hormone assays |
| Green | Heparin (10–20 IU/mL) | Activates antithrombin III (natural anticoagulant) | Osmotic Fragility Test (OFT), ABG, Cytogenetics |
| Lavender / Purple / Pink | K2 or K3 EDTA (1.5 mg/mL blood) | Chelates ionized calcium (Factor IV) | Complete Blood Count (CBC), DLC, Hb estimation |
| Grey | Sodium Fluoride \+ Potassium Oxalate | Inhibits enolase enzyme (halts glycolysis) | Blood Glucose estimation |
| Black | 3.8% Sodium Citrate | Binds calcium; 1:4 (citrate:blood ratio) | Erythrocyte Sedimentation Rate (Westergren ESR) |
