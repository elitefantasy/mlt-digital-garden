**Definition:**  
Electrophoresis is a laboratory technique used to **separate charged molecules** by their movement through a medium under the influence of an **electric field**.

### Principle
When an electric field is applied:
- **Positively charged molecules (cations)** move towards the **cathode (−)**
- **Negatively charged molecules (anions)** move towards the **anode (+)**.
- The rate of migration depends mainly on the molecule's **charge, size and shape**, as well as the properties of the supporting medium.

### Main components
1. **Power supply** – provides electric current.
2. **Electrophoresis chamber/tank** – contains the electrodes and buffer.
3. **Electrodes** – anode (+) and cathode (−).
4. **Buffer** – conducts electricity and maintains pH.
5. **Supporting medium** – agarose gel, polyacrylamide gel, cellulose acetate, etc.
6. **Sample** – DNA, RNA, proteins, or other charged molecules.
    
### Common types

|Type|Common use|
|---|---|
|**Agarose gel electrophoresis**|DNA/RNA separation|
|**PAGE**|Protein separation|
|**SDS-PAGE**|Protein separation mainly according to molecular size|
|**Agarose electrophoresis**|DNA fragments|
|**Cellulose acetate electrophoresis**|Serum proteins, hemoglobin|
|**Immunoelectrophoresis**|Identification of serum proteins using antigen-antibody reactions|

### Factors affecting migration
- **Molecular size**
- **Molecular charge**
- **Shape**
- **Electric field strength**
- **pH**
- **Buffer composition**
- **Gel concentration**
- **Temperature**
    

### Applications in MLT
- Separation of **DNA fragments**
- **Serum protein electrophoresis**
- **Hemoglobin electrophoresis**
- Detection of abnormal proteins
- Identification of molecular-weight differences
- Used as a step in techniques such as **Southern and Western blotting**


# Southern Blot Vs Western Blot
?

| Feature            | **Southern Blot**                                                  | **Western Blot**                                              |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Detects            | **DNA**                                                            | **Protein**                                                   |
| Developed by       | **Edwin Southern**                                                 | Name derived from Southern blot                               |
| Main purpose       | Detection of a **specific DNA sequence/gene**                      | Detection of a **specific protein**                           |
| Sample             | DNA                                                                | Protein                                                       |
| Separation         | **Agarose gel electrophoresis**                                    | **SDS-PAGE**                                                  |
| Transfer membrane  | Nylon/nitrocellulose                                               | Nitrocellulose/PVDF                                           |
| Detection molecule | **Labeled DNA/RNA probe**                                          | **Antibody**                                                  |
| Principle          | Complementary base pairing (**hybridization**)                     | **Antigen–antibody reaction**                                 |
| Detection          | Radioactive, fluorescent or chemiluminescent probe                 | Usually enzyme-linked antibody → chemiluminescence/color      |
| Applications       | Gene detection, DNA fingerprinting, mutation/rearrangement studies | Protein identification, expression studies, disease diagnosis |
