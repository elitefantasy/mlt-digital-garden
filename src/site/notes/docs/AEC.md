# Absolute Eosinophil Count (AEC)
- actual number of easinophil present in blood
- expressed as **cells/µL** or **× `10^9/L`
- AEC is calculated as $\text{AEC} = \frac{\text{Total leukocyte count} \times \text{Eosinophil percentage}}{100}$
- Normal Range
	- Easinophil in DLC : 1-5%
	- AEC : 0500 cell/µL
- Material Required: Easinophil diluting fluid(phloxine B or eosin, glycerol, DW, buffer)
- Fluid principle: Red blood cells are lysed, while eosinophil granules stain bright red or orange-red. Eosinophils can then be identified and counted in the counting chamber.
- The general hemocytometer formula is: 
	- $\text{Cells/µL} = \frac{\text{Number of cells counted} \times \text{Dilution factor}}{\text{Area counted} \times \text{Depth}}$
	- $\text{Cells/µL} = \frac{N \times D}{A \times 0.1}$
	- $\text{AEC} = N \times 50$
